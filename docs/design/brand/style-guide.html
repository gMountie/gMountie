I Voice & tone How gMountie talks.

Three rules.

Warmth, but earned. Charm comes from honest detail and respect for the reader's time — never from cleverness for its own sake.

i. Numbers beat adjectives.

"Lightning fast" is a feeling we owe the reader, and a number is the receipt. Write the number when you have it; rewrite when you don't.

Don't
"Lightning fast — built with gRPC for speedy communication."
Do
"Cold readdir in 12ms over a 60ms RTT. gRPC with Snappy compression on the wire."

ii. Earnest, never cute for cute's sake.

Warmth comes from craft, not from emoji clusters or exclamation points. One emoji per page, maximum. None in code or CLI output.

Don't
"Built with ❤️ and lots of ☕ — gMountie is your filesystem's best friend! 🤠⚡🔥"
Do
"A small server you run beside your data, and a small client you run on your laptop. That is the whole architecture."

iii. Speak about the tool, not the team.

Lead with what gMountie does, not what "we" did. Save first-person plural for the team page and release notes. Never use it for product copy.

Don't
"We built gMountie because we love distributed filesystems. We hope you'll love it too."
Do
"gMountie mounts a remote directory as a FUSE filesystem over gRPC. No VPN, no S3 layer, no rsync."

V Iconography Cartographic glyphs.

Stroke-based. Hand-feel. Drawn like a survey marker.

Icons are line-only, drawn on a 24px grid with a 1.5px stroke. The aesthetic reference is mid-century field-survey notation: simple geometric shapes that survive being printed on rough paper.

Grid

24×24, drawn on a half-pixel grid. Optical balance over perfect symmetry.

Stroke

1.5px at base, never thinner than 1.2. Rounded caps and joins.

Fill

Outlined by default. Ember fill reserved for the focal element of each icon.

Density

Three elements per icon, maximum. Icons exist to label, not to illustrate.

VI Wordmark The signature gesture.

One lowercase g, doing all the work.

The wordmark is set in Fraunces with opsz 96, SOFT 30, weight 380, tracking -0.035em. The lowercase g is set in Ember — the brand's signature note. A faint contour line continues from the wordmark to the right, a topographic mark we carry through icons and section rules.

gMountie
Casing — LOCKED

Always lowercase g, capital M. gMountie in prose. gmountie in code, URLs, and binaries. GMOUNTIE_ for env vars.

The g is for gRPC

When asked, the lowercase g is the wire protocol. One sentence, true, technical. Don't invent other meanings.

Clear space

Reserve at least the height of the M-stem (≈ 0.7× cap height) on all sides. No element may enter that band.

Minimum size

14px on screen, 12pt in print. Below those sizes, use the monogram instead.

Do · Don't

Use the wordmark as drawn
gMountie

Set in Fraunces, ember g, on paper or ink background.

Don't restyle the casing
GMountie

Never PascalCase. Never all-caps. Never drop the g.

Reverse on dark
gMountie

On ink, use Ember and Paper-Cream. Maintain stroke contrast.

Don't paint the g a different accent
gMountie

The g is always Ember. Never lake, moss, or terracotta.

VII Monogram Below 14px, use this.

A broad-brimmed hat in silhouette.

At favicon scale the wordmark is unreadable. The monogram is a broad-brimmed campaign hat with a single ember band — recognizable at 16×16, distinct from a fedora or cowboy hat, and unmistakable in a tab strip.

64 · app icon

At 16px the chin-strap and hat-band detail drop away. The brim becomes a single heavy ember stroke; the crown is a solid silhouette. Recognizable from anywhere in the tab strip.

VIII CLI presence The brand at 80 columns.

The brand should survive a monospace terminal.

Use box-drawing characters and a single ember-amber tint (terminal yellow/orange) when colors are available; degrade cleanly to pure mono when they are not.

┌─ gMountie ───────────────────────────────────────────┐ │ │ │ a network filesystem for anywhere. │ │ the g is for gRPC · github.com/gmountie │ │ │ ├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤ │ ▲ volume photos │ │ mounted ~/photos │ │ server fs.example.com:8443 │ │ latency 14ms cache cold state ready │ │ │ └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Rules: never animate. Never use spinners with more than two frames. The ember tint is the only color CLI output should reach for; everything else is the terminal's default. If NO_COLOR is set, render in pure monochrome.

IX Illustration The mascot, later.

Field journal sketches. Alpine marmot. Earned cuteness.

Phase 3 brings the actual mascot. This phase only sets the direction: hand-drawn line work, restrained color, an alpine animal who has clearly walked here.

The species — marmot

Alpine, plump, calm. Not a chipmunk (too cute), not a beaver (too Canadian-literal). A marmot reads as steady, watchful, native to the hills the brand evokes.

The uniform — hat & saddlebag

Broad-brimmed felt campaign hat with a single ember band. A leather saddlebag with a folded paper inside. Never RCMP red serge. Never anthropomorphic clothing beyond the hat and bag.

Line — drawn, not vectored

Hand-drawn line work scanned in. Loose contour lines around clean shapes. The line wobbles slightly; it is not perfect. Stroke weight varies from 1px to 2px.

Palette — paper, ink, one ember

The mascot lives in the same three-color world as the rest of the brand: paper cream background, deep ink line, one ember band or spot. No gradients. No drop shadows beyond a thin contour.

Posture — earned, never posing

The marmot is in the act of doing something — walking with the bag, looking up from a stamp, dipping a pen. Never centered, never smiling at the camera.

The sketch above is a wireframe placeholder — Phase 3 will bring the real character. The shape, hat, bag, and palette are the binding decisions.

X System Wordmark + monogram + lockups.

Three primary lockups, used by context.

The brand presents itself differently in a navbar, a favicon strip, a slide title, and a t-shirt. The system gives each a defined form.

01 · WORDMARK
gMountie

Default form. Use anywhere with at least 14px of available height.

02 · MONOGRAM

Favicons, app icons, social avatars. Anywhere the wordmark won't fit.

03 · HORIZONTAL LOCKUP
gMountie

Conference talks, slide titles, marketing headers. Maintain 0.3× M-height gap between mark and wordmark.

04 · STACK · FUTURE (PHASE 3)
gMountie

Future mascot + wordmark stack. Reserved for community-facing surfaces (README hero, swag) once the marmot ships.

XI End What this is. What it isn't.

Phase 1 of three.

This book covers brand strategy + design language. Phase 2 finalizes the full identity system: token files, icon set, illustration library, asset exports. Phase 3 brings the mascot artwork to life. Both build on these tokens; neither overrules them without a written reason.
